Getting started

How to play Daily Droplet

Each day you receive three base letters — two consonants and a vowel — arranged in a fixed order. Your goal is to build as many valid English words as possible that contain those three letters as an ordered sequence.

You can add letters before, between, or after the base letters using the on-screen keyboard. Drag letters into any gap in the word zone, or tap to append them to the end. Once you're happy with a word, it scores automatically.

Step 01
Get your three letters
Every day at midnight UTC, a new set of base letters is revealed. Everyone in the world plays the same puzzle that day.
Step 02
Build words
Drag letters from the keyboard into the word zone. The base letters must appear in order somewhere in your word — add as many extra letters as you like around them.
Step 03
Score & chain
Valid words score points. Build chain bonuses by extending a word you've already scored — adding one more letter to the end gives you a multiplier.
Step 04
Finish & share
When you're satisfied with your score, hit Finish. Your result is saved and you can share it with friends — one attempt per day.
Points system

How scoring works

Every valid word you find scores at least one point. Longer words and chain sequences reward more ambitious play, and rare letters like J, X, Q, and Z add bonus points on top.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How are the daily letters chosen?
Each day's puzzle is hand-curated or algorithmically selected to ensure at least 1,300 possible valid words can be formed from the base letters. This guarantees every puzzle is solvable and rewarding, regardless of skill level.
Can I play more than once a day?
Each puzzle can only be played once per day per device. Once you finish and submit your score, your result is saved and you can see the end-of-day screen until the next puzzle drops. This keeps the leaderboard fair.
When does the daily puzzle reset?
A new puzzle becomes available at midnight UTC every day. For players in Australia (AEST/AEDT), that's 10:00 AM or 11:00 AM depending on daylight saving time.
What counts as a valid word?
Words must appear in the ENABLE dictionary (152,481 words), which covers standard English including many British and Australian spellings. The base letters must appear in order somewhere within the word — for example, if your base is T·A·R, then "star", "guitar", and "starboard" all qualify.
Is Daily Droplet free to play?
Yes, completely free. No account required, no downloads, no subscriptions. Just open the page and play.
Does it work on mobile?
Daily Droplet is designed for both desktop and mobile. On mobile, tap a key to add it to the end of your word, or hold and drag to insert it at a specific position.
How do chain bonuses work?
If you've already scored a word and then find a longer word that extends it by adding one or more letters, you earn a chain bonus. The bonus is 1 + (chain length × 2) points on top of the base word score. Chains reset if you attempt an invalid word, remove a letter, or press Clear.
